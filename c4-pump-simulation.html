The model

Two well-mixed CO2 pools and one C4 acid pool, integrated forward in time. Partial pressures are in µbar, fluxes in µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, conductances in mmol m⁻² s⁻¹ bar⁻¹, so a flux is g·ΔC/1000. The flux expressions are those of von Caemmerer and Furbank's C4 model; the time dependence is added here so the pump can be seen filling.

Vp = min( Vpmax·Cm/(Cm+Kp) , x·J/2 ) PEP carboxylation L = gbs·(Cbs − Cm)/1000 leak back to mesophyll Vc = min( Vcmax·Cbs/(Cbs+Kc(1+Obs/Ko)) , (1−x)·J/(3+3.5φo) ) φo = Vo/Vc = Obs/(Sc/o·Cbs) oxygenation ratio A = Vc − 0.5·Vo − Rd net assimilation d(Cm)/dt = [ gs(Ca−Cm)/1000 + L + Rm − Vp ] / βm d(P)/dt = Vp − kd·P C4 acid pool d(Cbs)/dt = [ kd·P + 0.5·Vo + Rbs − Vc − L ] / βbs

Leakiness is φ = L/Vp. The ATP bill is (3·Vc + 3.5·Vo + 2·Vp)/A: three per carboxylation, roughly three and a half per oxygenation recovered through the photorespiratory cycle, two per turn of the C4 cycle to regenerate PEP from pyruvate. With the pump matched to demand this lands near five, which is the textbook number.

Constants at 25 °C: Kp 80 µbar, Kc 650 µbar, Ko 450 mbar, Sc/o 2590, Vcmax 70, Rd 1.0 split evenly between the two cells, gs 150 mmol m⁻² s⁻¹ bar⁻¹, x 0.4, Jmax 360 µmol ATP m⁻² s⁻¹ on a non-rectangular hyperbola (α 0.24, θ 0.72). Bundle sheath O2 is raised above ambient by residual PSII activity there, Obs = O + 0.15·A/(0.047·gbs). At the default settings the leaf sits close to co-limitation: PEP carboxylation is capped by ATP supply and Rubisco by its own capacity, which is where a well-lit maize leaf actually operates.

Simplifications, stated deliberately
  • The C4 acid shuttle is one lumped pool with first-order decarboxylation. Malate and aspartate are not distinguished, PEP and pyruvate pools are not tracked, and plasmodesmatal transport is not resolved.
  • One decarboxylation type only, NADP-ME-like. Switching to NAD-ME or PCK would move reductant between compartments and change bundle sheath O2.
  • The bundle sheath is a single well-mixed compartment. There is no radial gradient inside the cell and no separate chloroplast envelope resistance, so the leak is governed by one conductance.
  • Stomatal and mesophyll conductance are lumped into a fixed gs. Stomata do not respond to Ci, so the water-use argument for C4 cannot be made here.
  • Carbonic anhydrase is assumed fast and non-limiting; PEPC kinetics are written in CO2 rather than HCO3− terms with an apparent Kp.
  • Temperature is fixed at 25 °C. Every kinetic constant is a 25 °C value, so the C3/C4 crossover with temperature is outside this model.
  • Buffering capacities βm and βbs are chosen to give roughly one-second time constants. They set how fast the traces move and nothing else; steady states are independent of them.
  • Light limitation is handled as an ATP budget with fixed partitioning x = 0.4 to the C4 cycle. Real partitioning shifts with conditions.
  • Triose phosphate export, starch synthesis and sink limitation are absent, so the leaf never becomes sink-limited.

Sources for the flux structure and constants: von Caemmerer & Furbank (1999) and von Caemmerer (2000); leakiness and its isotopic estimate follow Farquhar (1983); the ATP stoichiometry is Hatch's.

Things worth trying
  • Drag leakiness from 5 to 20. Net fixation falls by about a fifth while the ATP bill climbs, because the pump keeps spending at the same rate on CO2 that escapes before Rubisco sees it. This is the whole argument for a suberised wall.
  • Switch the pump off. The leaf does not become a C3 leaf; it becomes a broken C4 leaf, with Rubisco sealed inside a compartment that CO2 can barely reach. Compare it with the C3 reference row, which is the same Rubisco working at mesophyll CO2.
  • Take air O2 to 20 mbar and watch the C3 reference climb while the C4 leaf barely moves. Suppressing oxygenation is what the pump was already doing.
  • Walk air CO2 from 180 to 900 µbar. The C4 leaf saturates near 400; the C3 reference keeps rising. Read off where the advantage was largest and where it is heading.
  • Move light to 250 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹. The C4 leaf now falls below the C3 reference, because two of its five ATP go on a pump that a shaded leaf cannot afford.
  • Push pump capacity past 80 and nothing happens. Find out why in the limitation line under the headline numbers, then raise the light and try again.

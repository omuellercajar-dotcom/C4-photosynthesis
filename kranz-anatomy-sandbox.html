Diagnosis

    The model

    Two well-mixed compartments. Each holds a CO2 pool, a C4 acid pool and a 3-carbon acid pool, and every enzyme acts only in the compartment you put it in. Partial pressures are in µbar, fluxes in µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, conductances in mmol m⁻² s⁻¹ bar⁻¹.

    in cell c, if the enzyme is there: Vp(c) = Vpmax · fCA(c) · C(c)/(C(c)+Kp) · C3(c)/(C3(c)+Kc3) Vme(c) = Vmemax · Ma(c)/(Ma(c)+Kma) Vc(c) = Vcmax · C(c)/(C(c)+Kc(1+O/Ko)), Vo(c) = Vc(c)·O/(Sc/o·C(c)) fCA(c) = 1 with carbonic anhydrase in the same cell, else 0.15 ATP: demand = 3ΣVc + 3.5ΣVo + 2ΣVp; if demand > J every rate is scaled by J/demand CO2: only the mesophyll exchanges with the airspace, gs(Ca − Cm) across the wall, L = gbs(Cbs − Cm) shuttle: kpd·(Ma_M − Ma_BS) and kpd·(C3_M − C3_BS)

    The C4 acid and 3-carbon pools together are conserved, so an arrangement that makes acid without consuming it runs the shuttle dry and stops. Net assimilation is ΣVc − 0.5ΣVo − Rd, and at steady state it equals the flux in through the stomata, which is the model's own consistency check.

    Constants at 25 °C: Kp 80 µbar, Kc 650 µbar, Ko 450 mbar, Sc/o 2590, Vcmax 70, Vpmax 80, Vme 90, Rd 1.0 split between the cells, gs 150, Jmax 360 µmol ATP m⁻² s⁻¹, shuttle pool 150 µmol m⁻².

    Simplifications, stated deliberately
    • PEP regeneration is folded into PEP carboxylase: the 2 ATP for pyruvate,Pi dikinase are charged on every carboxylation, and PPDK is not a separate piece. In a lumped two-compartment model its placement makes no difference to any flux, so offering it as a choice would have been dishonest.
    • One decarboxylation type, NADP-ME-like. NAD-ME and PCK move reductant differently between the cells, which this model does not represent.
    • Malate and aspartate are not distinguished, and neither are PEP and pyruvate; each is one lumped pool per cell.
    • ATP is one leaf-wide budget shared by both compartments. In a real NADP-ME leaf the bundle sheath chloroplasts lack photosystem II and make ATP by cyclic flow, so the two compartments do not draw on the same pool as freely as this.
    • Bundle sheath O2 is held at the ambient value. Residual photosystem II activity there would raise it and increase oxygenation.
    • Carbonic anhydrase is represented as a switch on PEP carboxylase's apparent rate rather than by modelling the CO2–HCO3− equilibrium. The uncatalysed value of 0.15 is illustrative.
    • Plasmodesmatal exchange is first order in the concentration difference, with one constant for both metabolites. The shuttle pool size is chosen to give visible gradients, not measured.
    • Temperature is fixed at 25 °C, stomatal conductance is fixed and does not respond to Ci, and there is no sink limitation.

    Flux structure and constants follow von Caemmerer and Furbank; the arrangement of enzymes between compartments follows Hatch's account of the C4 pathway.

    Things worth trying
    • Build the correct leaf, then move the decarboxylase into the mesophyll. Capture and release now happen in the same compartment. Watch the C4 acid arrow keep moving while net fixation collapses, and read the wasted ATP.
    • Do the same again but with the light at 400 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹. In bright light the leaf can afford a futile cycle; in shade it cannot, and the Calvin cycle starves.
    • Put Rubisco in the mesophyll and leave the pump running. The bundle sheath fills to an absurd pressure because nothing there consumes CO₂, and none of it helps.
    • Move carbonic anhydrase into the bundle sheath. The pathway is topologically correct and still performs worse than no pump at all, because PEP carboxylase needs bicarbonate and nothing is making it fast enough.
    • Build the correct leaf, then take the plasmodesmata down to 0.3. The enzymes are all in the right places and the leaf still fails, because the shuttle cannot carry the flux.
    • Put all four enzymes in the bundle sheath. Nothing works, and the reason is anatomy rather than biochemistry.
